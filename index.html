Flagship · open source

jobradar: an AI data pipeline you can audit

A small pipeline that runs on a laptop. It collects job ads from public European APIs, uses local models to classify them and extract facts, and matches them against your CV, which never leaves your machine. Every step emits OpenLineage events, every table is plain Delta Lake, and governance (data contracts, retention, privacy scrubbing, lineage-driven erasure) is built in rather than bolted on.
