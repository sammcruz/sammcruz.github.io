About me

I'm from Brazil. I grew up there and studied Naval and Oceanic Engineering at UFRJ in Rio de Janeiro. My career started at sea level: satellite images, ocean temperature and currents, and a research stay at NOAA in Miami. Working with all that data pulled me into analytics, BI and, in the end, data engineering.

On 2024 I moved to Barcelona, where I worked at Glovo, a food delivery app, then I worked as a data engineer at isolutions, teach Databricks, speak at conferences and meetups, and write about what I learn. This site is where it all comes together.

Flagship · open source

jobradar: an AI data pipeline you can audit

A small pipeline that runs on a laptop. It collects job ads from public European APIs, uses local models to classify them and extract facts, and matches them against your CV, which never leaves your machine. Every step emits OpenLineage events, every table is plain Delta Lake, and governance (data contracts, retention, privacy scrubbing, lineage-driven erasure) is built in rather than bolted on.
